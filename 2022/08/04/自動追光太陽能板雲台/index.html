自動追光太陽能板雲台

自動追光太陽能板雲台

作品簡介

此作品是一個基於 Arduino 開發的 自動追光太陽能板雲台。其設計目的是讓太陽能板能夠自動追蹤光源方向，確保太陽能板隨時正對陽光，從而達到最大的發電效率。通過光敏電阻感應光線強度的變化，該雲台系統能自動調整方向，實現對光源的追蹤。

展示影片

元件與結構

  • 感測器架構：雲台上安裝了四個光敏電阻，分別位於太陽能板的四個角落。這些光敏電阻的作用是檢測光線在不同方向的強度，並向控制系統反饋數據。當某一方向的光線較強時，該方向的光敏電阻會檢測到較高的光強度值。

  • 控制系統：系統使用 Arduino 作為控制單元，負責接收光敏電阻的數據，並計算各方向的光線強度差異。根據這些數據，Arduino 將指令傳遞給伺服馬達，調整雲台的角度，讓太陽能板朝向光線最強的方向。

  • 伺服馬達調整：該雲台配備了兩個伺服馬達，分別控制水平方向和垂直方向的旋轉。Arduino 根據光敏電阻的光強度差異來控制伺服馬達的運動，從而實現雲台的雙軸旋轉，讓太陽能板能夠朝向陽光最強的位置。

  • 自動追光：當太陽移動時，光敏電阻檢測到的光強度會隨之變化，Arduino 會根據光線的變化實時調整伺服馬達的角度，讓太陽能板始終保持對準太陽的方向。

Sun-Tracking Solar Panel Gimbal

Overview

This project is an Arduino-based sun-tracking solar panel gimbal. It is designed to keep a solar panel automatically tracking the light source so the panel always faces the sun directly, achieving maximum power-generation efficiency. Photoresistors sense changes in light intensity, and the gimbal automatically adjusts its orientation to follow the light.

Demo Video

Components & Structure

  • Sensor layout: Four photoresistors are mounted at the four corners of the solar panel. They detect light intensity from different directions and feed the readings back to the control system — when light is stronger from one direction, that photoresistor reports a higher value.

  • Control system: An Arduino serves as the control unit, receiving the photoresistor data and computing the light-intensity differences between directions. Based on this data, it commands the servo motors to adjust the gimbal's angle so the panel faces the strongest light.

  • Servo adjustment: The gimbal is equipped with two servo motors controlling horizontal and vertical rotation. The Arduino drives them according to the intensity differences, achieving two-axis rotation so the panel points toward the brightest direction.

  • Automatic tracking: As the sun moves, the detected light intensities change, and the Arduino adjusts the servo angles in real time so the solar panel always stays aligned with the sun.
